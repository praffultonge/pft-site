Law tells you the rule. This is about what the rule does — to capital, to structure, and to the people who have to live with both.

Analysis of regulatory frameworks, capital structures, and the second and third-order effects of legal design. Written for practitioners, investors, and anyone who reads the footnotes. The focus is GIFT City IFSC, Indian capital markets, and the cross-border structures that connect them to the world. Occasionally, something further afield.

No summaries. No checklists. No congratulations to the law for existing.

Provided further that is the clause lawyers use when the paragraph before it almost got it right. It qualifies, it carves out, it complicates. It seemed like the right name for a publication that treats every answer as the beginning of a better question.

Written from GIFT City IFSC, where the regulations are new enough that the exceptions are still being written.
